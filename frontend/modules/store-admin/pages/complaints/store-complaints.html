Complaints & Grievance System Process

1. Anonymous Whistleblower Safeguard

By toggling "Submit Anonymously", all database links to the submitter's user profile, username, or IP are strictly removed. The system saves the report under a randomized key (e.g. TK-ANO-XXXXXX) allowing zero tracing.

2. Target Authority Routing

The report is routed directly to the selected authority (Store Admin, Regional Manager, National HR, or Executive Compliance). It will only appear on that specific target level's secure dashboard.

3. Resolution & Finding Publication

Investigators review the details and update the status to "Under Investigation". Once concluded, their official investigation reports are published back to the reporter's key for secure retrieval.

4. Multi-Level Escalation Path

If resolution is delayed or unsatisfactory, the reporter can escalate the ticket directly to the next higher level of authority (Store $\rightarrow$ Regional $\rightarrow$ National $\rightarrow$ Executive) via the Tracking tab.
