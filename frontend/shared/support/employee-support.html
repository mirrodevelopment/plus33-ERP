Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q: When is monthly salary disbursed?

Salary is direct deposited on the 28th of every month. Overtime rates apply at 1.5x on standard weekdays and 2.0x on national holidays.

Q: How does Anonymous Whistleblower Protection work?

When toggled ON for Workplace Grievances or HR issues, all user identifiers are stripped. You get a 12-char tracking key to track executive investigation findings.

Complaints & Grievance System Process

1. Anonymous Whistleblower Safeguard

By toggling "Submit Anonymously", all database links to your user profile, username, or IP are strictly removed. The system saves the report under a randomized key (e.g. TK-ANO-XXXXXX) allowing zero tracing.

2. Target Authority Routing

Your report is routed directly to the selected authority (Store Admin, Regional Manager, National HR, or Executive Compliance). It will only appear on that specific target level's secure dashboard.

3. Resolution & Finding Publication

Investigators review the details and update the status to "Under Investigation". Once concluded, their official investigation reports are published back to your key for secure retrieval.

4. Multi-Level Escalation Path

If resolution is delayed or unsatisfactory, you can escalate the ticket directly to the next higher level of authority (Store $\rightarrow$ Regional $\rightarrow$ National $\rightarrow$ Executive) via the Tracking tab.
